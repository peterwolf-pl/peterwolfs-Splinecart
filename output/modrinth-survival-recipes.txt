peterwolf's Splinecart — Survival Crafting Recipes

All four player-obtainable items have shaped crafting recipes in Survival. The Track Follower is a hidden entity spawned by tracks and is not crafted.

---

Track Ties
Description: Interact to rotate when placed.

Unlocks when you pick up a Stick or any Wooden Slab.

Crafting (makes 8):
  [Wooden Slab]   [Wooden Slab]
  [Wooden Slab] [Stick] [Wooden Slab]
  [Wooden Slab]   [Wooden Slab]

Any wooden slab works in every slot marked Wooden Slab.

---

Track
Description: Links two Track Ties blocks together.

Unlocks when you pick up a Rail or Iron Block.

Crafting (makes 4):
      [Iron Block]
        [Rail]
      [Iron Block]

---

Chain Drive Track
Description: Track variant that pulls minecarts.

Unlocks when you pick up an Activator Rail, Chain, or Iron Block.

Crafting (makes 4):
      [Iron Block]
  [Chain] [Activator Rail] [Chain]
      [Iron Block]

---

Magnetic Track
Description: Track variant that accelerates or slows minecarts.

Unlocks when you pick up a Powered Rail, Iron Block, or Copper Block.

Crafting (makes 4):
      [Iron Block]
 [Copper Block] [Powered Rail] [Copper Block]
      [Iron Block]

The magnetic track's strength follows the redstone signal powering its Track Ties block.

---

Quick start

1. Craft Track Ties and place them on any face of a block.
2. Right-click a tie to rotate it. The dark arrows show the forward direction.
3. Place a second tie facing the route you want.
4. Craft a Track item, use it on the first tie, then on the second tie.
5. Run a vanilla minecart into the first tie to start riding the spline.
